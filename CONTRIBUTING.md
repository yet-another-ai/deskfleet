# Contributing to DeskFleet

DeskFleet welcomes code, documentation, and design contributions, as well as
reviews and bug reports.

## Getting started

Follow the [development guide](agents/docs/environment.md) to install the
locked mise toolchain, start the services you need, and find focused checks.
Start with the [agent guide](AGENTS.md) for repository conventions that apply to
all contributors. Consult the [architecture guide](agents/docs/architecture.md)
when changing component boundaries or resource ownership and the
[CI guide](agents/docs/ci.md) for validation and the required Gate check.

Update the document that owns any behavior or rule you change. Engineering
documentation belongs in `agents/docs/`. The `docs/` directory holds the
product website and user manual. Create or accept an architecture decision
record only at a maintainer's request.

## Contributor License Agreement

Each contributor must have a recorded acceptance of the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) with **Yet Another AI Global Ltd.** before
their contributions are merged. The CLA lets contributors retain ownership while
granting copyright and patent permissions, including the ability to sublicense
contributions under different license terms. The Work's current public license
is [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). If an employer owns the relevant rights, obtain its
authorization or have an authorized representative accept on its behalf.
Identify third-party material and its license separately.

### Signing the CLA

1. Read [CLA.md](CLA.md). Use GitHub's file permalink to record the exact commit
   containing the agreement You are accepting.
2. Email [contact@yetanother.ai](mailto:contact@yetanother.ai) with the subject
   `DeskFleet CLA acceptance`. Attach a copy of that agreement and include the
   completed acceptance below in the email body. Send it from the contact email
   listed in Your acceptance.
3. Link Your pull request in the email. A maintainer will confirm receipt and
   record the GitHub account's CLA status on the pull request. Keep a copy of the
   email and agreement for Your records.

```text

below with the named Recipient. I intend this email and my typed signature to
constitute my electronic signature to that Agreement. If signing for a legal
entity, I am authorized to bind it.

Recipient legal name: Yet Another AI Global Ltd.
Agreement version and immutable commit URL:
Contributor legal name (individual or legal entity):
Contact email:
GitHub username(s) covered:
Pull request URL:
Other earlier contributions expressly included, if any (otherwise "none"):
Signer's full legal name:
Signer's title, if signing for a legal entity:
Signature (type full legal name):
Date (YYYY-MM-DD):
```

Send the acceptance privately; do not commit it or post personal details in a
public issue or pull request. A maintainer should confirm account ownership
through the corresponding GitHub account before recording acceptance. For
company contributions, record the authorized accounts and the company's scope
of ownership. Contributions outside that scope need the relevant rights owner's
acceptance. Reuse a recorded acceptance for later contributions covered by the
same agreement and authority; changes to the agreement require a new acceptance.

Maintainers retain the acceptance email, the exact agreement text and commit,
the signature date, and the account verification together in private records.
Only the GitHub account and signing status need to appear on the pull request.
Checking signatures is manual; this repository has no automated CLA check.

## Preparing a pull request

- Use a [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) title such
  as `fix(daemon): preserve session state` or `docs: update the setup guide`.
- Keep each pull request focused on a single goal. Follow the existing style
  and architecture, and keep unrelated refactors and formatting out of the diff.
- Explain the problem, resulting behavior, and any affected boundaries. Run
  checks appropriate to the change with the locked mise toolchain and include
  the results.
- Confirm that you have the rights to submit the material and identify
  third-party material and its license as required by the CLA.
- Link a tracking issue when proposing an exception to a documented policy or
  deferring a known correctness fix.

## AI-assisted and AI-generated contributions

DeskFleet welcomes contributions written with or generated by AI. We apply the
same standards for correctness, maintainability, and review to every
contribution.

### Human responsibility and review

Every commit or pull request that uses AI must have a human owner who can
explain the change in review, fix defects, and take responsibility for the
result.

Before merge, at least one person who meets those expectations must review the
change in substance. Check its intent, the boundaries it touches, and how it
can fail. When AI adds or changes tests, a human must check that the assertions
cover the intended behavior and preserve existing coverage.

If the author has done the only review, prefer a second reviewer who knows the
affected subsystem, especially for changes to Team and Machine isolation,
authorization or credentials, Electron preload and IPC, or unsafe Rust, FFI,
and native bindings.

### Attribution and disclosure

The accountable person should be the committer. AI attribution in commit
trailers and disclosure in the pull request description are optional. Review
and merge decisions should be based on the contribution itself, regardless of
whether the author disclosed AI use.

### Maintainer discretion

Maintainers may decline or defer submissions based on their scope, quality, or
the time needed to review them. This includes work reserved for newcomers,
changes that are too large to review or conflict with documented architecture,
duplicate or already-decided proposals, and speculative or spam-like batches.
Maintainers make the final decision on whether to take on a submission.

## Filing issues

Search [existing issues](https://github.com/yet-another-ai/deskfleet/issues)
before opening a report or proposal. For bugs, include steps to reproduce,
expected and actual behavior, and the DeskFleet version and platform where
relevant. For a proposal, describe the problem and the outcome you need. If you
use AI to draft an issue, verify that the details are accurate and actionable
before posting.
